## Evidence rules

1. A concept becomes *implemented* when an archived executable module and reproducible inputs are present; a description or UI does not suffice.
2. Results from numerical simulations are not real optical, biomedical, electrochemical, robotics or biological measurements.
3. A recorded optimization is not automatically inherited by another version; baselines, workloads and uncertainty constraints travel with the result.
4. Family labels (forest, fungal, DNA, genetic, species, thought) name engineering analogies and mechanisms; they are not measurements of consciousness or biological growth.
5. FireClay / Flux materials are logged as requirements and research only; no private client documents, account access, credentials or unrelated corporate records are released.
