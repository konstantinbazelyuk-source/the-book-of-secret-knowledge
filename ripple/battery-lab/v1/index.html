4. Published calcium cells

Ca metal / PTCDI: 1.7 V discharge, initial 105 mAh/g cathode, room-temperature CE stabilizing around 67%. Source: ACS Materials Letters 2025.

Computed 178.5 Wh/kg of cathode active mass only from V×Q approximation. This is not measured complete cell energy density.

Ca metal / carbon: >500 cycles and 85.4% capacity retention reported for a different engineered interface. Advanced Science 2025.

5. Real engineering gates

Atomic: prove calcium chemistry, reversible Ca²⁺ reactions, electrolyte interphase, and actual cathode redox chemistry.

Instrument: record controlled charge/discharge, impedance, temperature, and calibrated uncertainty.

Independent confirmation: reproduce across cells and separate false capacity from electrolyte side reactions.

Calcium-metal chemistry belongs in a qualified electrochemistry lab, not an unprotected home-build rechargeable pack. A certified battery module can already power Ripple while the Ca chemistry is researched.
