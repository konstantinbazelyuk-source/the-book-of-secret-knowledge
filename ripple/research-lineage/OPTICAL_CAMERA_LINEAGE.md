## Recoverable implementation process

Light sensor samples are tagged with sampling time and units; derived RGB/contrast becomes task-specialized neuron inputs. Spike events remain software signals and are not themselves EKG/EEG. Colony scheduling reads recorded demand and runs real image-processing workers. FieldSphere magnetic values retain µT units and separate calibration/geometry metadata, never silently converted into RGB. Neuronal spike markers may authorize bounded Golf jobs only via explicit allowlists. Save configuration/state with the evidence type (measured/synthetic/calculated) and test the downstream output under disable/input ablations.
