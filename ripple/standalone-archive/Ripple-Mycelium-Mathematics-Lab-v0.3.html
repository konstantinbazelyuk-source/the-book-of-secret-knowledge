3 / Evidence and limitations

Learning: distributed logistic-regression gradients on synthetic numeric examples. Product: checked integer-cents order totals, inventory shortfalls. Study: inverse-variance aggregation with independent-variance assumptions and duplicate source IDs. These are real calculations on synthetic input, not verified factual research or model-level cognition.

Cost warning: the worker-seconds and pulse rates are simulated. The 720 trials explicitly assume a matched specialist uses 1 spike per part, a generalist 2, and a mismatched specialist 3. The cost of link transfer is modeled as fixed hop delay; no link congestion, real power, or physical neuron has been measured.

Scientific foundation: Integrate-and-fire dynamics, work stealing, replica autoscaling, fungal-electrical-signal research limitations.
