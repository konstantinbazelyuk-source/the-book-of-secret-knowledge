2 · Calcium cell AC impedance (EIS)

A small AC perturbation of a separately biased electrochemical cell helps characterize internal impedance. This assumed Randles model contains series electrolyte resistance, double-layer capacitance, charge-transfer resistance and optional semi-infinite Warburg diffusion. No calcium-chemistry parameters are fabricated.
