128 field trials · no injected gray descendant

Gray fields are not gray genes

32 fixed holdout seeds across baseline, occlusion, occupancy density, and combined modes, 240 steps on 20×16 fields. Previously defined event threshold: ≥15% of sufficiently bright traversable cells for eight consecutive steps.

Observed in this numerical setup: no sustained field-monochrome event. This does not demonstrate that color diffusion can never produce gray; it only rules out this outcome under these tested conditions.

Emergence-event false-positive audit

A field initialized already gray makes the original detector signal an event after eight steps. Alpha 1.1 introduces an independent audit requiring a previously non-gray interval before recording a subsequent sustained gray transition. A forced gray transition is a positive control, not spontaneous emergence.

Camera-relative color transmittance study

No real-camera frames were available during this analysis. A ready-to-run computer-webcam capture tool and fail-closed verifier are included in the full research folder.

  1. Fix camera, sample, lamp and central region of interest in a darkened/controlled room.
  2. Manually lock exposure, ISO, white balance, focus and illumination; record the settings.
  3. Capture at least four dark/blocked, four clear/unobstructed and four material/test frames, without moving the rig or changing light.
  4. Reject clipped channels, weak dark/clear contrast, changing metadata or unstable replicates.
  5. Use the output only for estimated relative RGB transmittance. Confirm physical transmittance with a calibrated optical instrument before scientific measurement claims.
python research/capture_webcam.py \
 --geometry fixed-rig-01 --illumination stable-led-01 \
 --exposure-ms 10 --iso 100 --white-balance fixed-5000K \
 --confirm-manual-exposure --confirm-manual-white-balance

node research/evaluate-camera.mjs ripple-measured-camera-session.json result.json
The command's exposure/ISO numbers are examples; replace them with your actually verified fixed camera settings. A webcam's processed RGB pixels are not spectrally resolved radiometry.
