Source research

Diffusion and emergent pattern research follows published reaction–diffusion mathematics (Turing, 1952; Tyson, 2026 review). The Alpha engine implements an intentionally simpler, conservative three-channel transport field with explicit source injection; it does not represent a demonstrated Turing instability. All simulation settings and reproducible test data are packaged with this release.
