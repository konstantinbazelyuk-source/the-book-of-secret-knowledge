Distinguishing three scientific claims

1. Wave optics: predicts a smooth intensity pattern from phase-coherent scalar amplitudes. 2. Photon statistics: localized detection events sampled from that expected distribution. 3. Neuron dynamics: numerical pulse encoding using copies of the unchanged Ripple integrate-and-fire class. These are separate models joined by declared observation interfaces.

The plane-wave field calculation is conditional: I=0.5 c epsilon0 E_peak² and B_peak=E_peak/c for an ideal plane wave in vacuum. An RGB frame cannot determine an instantaneous optical E(t) or B(t), and visibility zero contains no information about slit separation in this model.

Experiment status: theoretical computations, synthetic Poisson/multinomial samples, and offline Node.js neuron execution. External sensors: not connected. Neurodevelopment: conceptual ON/OFF coding analogue only, not a growing biological neuron.
