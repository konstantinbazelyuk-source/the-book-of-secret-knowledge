Does it recover after brightness changes?

120 simulated seconds. Red intensity changes from 0.35 to 0.70 at 40 s, then returns to 0.35 at 80 s. Both neurons see identical input. One adapts; the other keeps its initial threshold. This test runs immediately, without waiting two minutes.

A real bench build

Optics + electronics
1 / Transmit the inputs

A common-anode RGB LED, powered from USB 5 V. Three 2N3904 transistor branches control its channels through 1 kΩ current-limiting resistors.

2 / Measure the spectrum

An Adafruit AS7341 breakout measures eight visible bands. Dark and single-color references form a calibrated decoder. Saturated readings are rejected.

3 / Regulate the output

A Raspberry Pi Pico runs the same Python neuron core. USB serial carries the modeled event count; the onboard LED indicates activity.

The hardware measures spectral intensity. Neuron dynamics and feedback are implemented electronically. It does not measure the instantaneous electric or magnetic field of light. Task learning, a neuron network, and a motor controller are later engineering stages.
