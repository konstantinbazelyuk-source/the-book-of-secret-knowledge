## Earliest-source gap

An item referred to as `Ripple-Cybernetic-Mycelium-v0.zip` was a search lead. The earliest complete Mycelium **program actually examined in this work** was `Ripple-Cybernetic-Mycelium-v0.1.zip`, recovered verbatim inside `01-ripple-neuron-math-core.originals-01.zip`. A separate prior `v0.zip` implementation or an earlier biological colonization ancestor has **not** been identified as an earlier independently verified source. Do not silently attribute subsequent threshold inversion or forest growth to v0.1.
